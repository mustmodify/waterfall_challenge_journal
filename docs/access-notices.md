# Access notices found in AllTrails, 2026-09-14

Twenty-one waterfalls we publish sit on a trail whose AllTrails record carries
an access condition. We hold none of them. Five were noticed by hand while
matching; the rest came out of the cached descriptions afterwards, which is the
argument for reading the cache rather than the screen.

**These are dated readings of somebody else's page, not facts.** Every one will
expire — a closure lifts, a fee changes, a road reopens — and a stale closure
costs a visitor a trip they could have taken. Nothing here is written into
`features`; it is a holding list, in the same spirit as
[hikingwnc-data-issues.md](hikingwnc-data-issues.md), and the shape it wants is
the `cautions` table discussed with JW: severity, source, observed_on, and a
re-check date.

Severity below is by what the visitor should *do*, not by how the source words it.
