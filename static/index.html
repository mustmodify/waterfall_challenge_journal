Welcome to Wanderfall!

A hobby project by jw.

This is a list of waterfalls and other quests in Western North Carolina. No account needed; if you do create one, you'll be able to track which features you visit and rate them.

Wanderfall also lists several challenges. Visit the entire list and you get a badge!

Here's how to read the map
  • blue is a waterfall
  • brown is a lookout tower
And when they are close together

A ring is a group of nearby places. The bigger it is, the more it holds — click one to zoom in and break it apart.

I enjoy making stuff like this, but REALLY enjoy making stuff that gets used. Send feedback! jw@mustmodify.com
