Welcome to Wanderfall!

A hobby project by jw.

This is a list of waterfalls and other amazing quests in Western North Carolina. No account needed; if you do create one, you'll be able to track which places you visit and rate them.

Wanderfall also lists several challenges. Visit the entire list and you get a badge!

Here's how to read the map
  • blue is a waterfall
  • sienna is a lookout tower
  • teal is a swimming hole
And when they are close together

A ring is a group of nearby places. The bigger it is, the more it holds — click one to zoom in and break it apart.

Coloring by Rating

Most of these waterfalls come with ratings from HikingWNC — how good they look, how well they photograph, and how much solitude you get. Pick one and every pin is shaded by it.

lowestbest

A group is sliced by what it holds, so you can tell a cluster of middling falls from one hiding a couple of great ones — without zooming into every single bubble.

Pick two — say Solitude and Beauty — and each fall is scored on the average of both. A stunning roadside fall full of people drops to the middle, and somewhere merely pretty that you'll have entirely to yourself climbs above it.

Anything without the rating you picked drops off the map rather than being drawn in a color implying a score it hasn't got. That includes every lookout tower, which have no ratings at all.
